{%- comment -%} Reading time in minutes for include.post. `number_of_words` splits on whitespace, which badly undercounts Chinese, so a post can opt into character-based counting with `lang: zh` in its front matter. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign _p = include.post -%} {%- if _p.lang == 'zh' or _p.lang == 'cn' or _p.lang == 'zh-CN' -%} {%- assign _chars = _p.content | strip_html | strip_newlines | replace: ' ', '' | size -%} {%- assign _min = _chars | divided_by: 400 | plus: 1 -%} {%- else -%} {%- assign _words = _p.content | number_of_words -%} {%- assign _min = _words | divided_by: 200 | plus: 1 -%} {%- endif -%} {{- _min -}}
